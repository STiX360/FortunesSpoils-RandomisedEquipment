# Bargain Affix Balance

Ordinary paired benefit/drawback affixes; sequences are T1 through T6.
Each occupies one affix slot, not both a prefix and a suffix.

| Family | Benefit | Drawback |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Winter Pact | Resist Frost: 4 / 6 / 9 / 12 / 15 / 20% | Weakness to Fire: 4 / 6 / 9 / 13 / 18 / 25% |
| Storm Pact | Resist Shock: 4 / 6 / 9 / 12 / 15 / 20% | Weakness to Frost: 4 / 6 / 9 / 13 / 18 / 25% |
| Borrowed Words | Speechcraft: 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 / 7 / 8 | Drain Strength: 2 / 3 / 4 / 6 / 8 / 10 |
| Scholar's Burden | Enchant: 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 / 7 / 8 | Drain Speed: 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 6 / 8 |
| Hobbling Fury | Target Drain Speed: 4 / 6 / 9 / 12 / 16 / 20 | Target Fortify Strength: 1 / 1 / 2 / 2 / 3 / 4 |
| Blinding Vigor | Target Blind: 5 / 8 / 12 / 18 / 25 / 35% | Target Fortify Agility: 1 / 1 / 2 / 2 / 3 / 4 |

Both components of strike bargains last three seconds. Other bargains remain CE.

## Resistance-Aware Balance

Values are nominal magnitudes before resistance. Resist Magicka can reduce
Blind, Drain Attribute and elemental Weakness effects. Elemental damage itself
uses the corresponding elemental resistance, not Resist Magicka.
See [UESP: Resist Magicka](https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Morrowind:Resist_Magicka).

Wearer-facing weaknesses and drains are deliberately noticeable without
resistance. Resistance investment may soften or eliminate them; no resistance
is assumed in their baseline balance. Drain Strength also costs carrying
capacity, so it is not scaled identically to Blind or Drain Speed.

On-strike Blind and Drain Speed are benefits against the target, not penalties
to the wearer. These received stronger magnitudes to remain relevant against
resistant enemies. Their accompanying Fortify Agility/Strength effects benefit
the enemy and are not reduced by Resist Magicka. At 50% resistance, T6 Blind
has a nominal effective magnitude of 17.5 and T6 Drain Speed of 10, while the
target still receives the full 4-point attribute buff.

No family supplies Resist Magicka to cancel its own debuff. Raw record-stat
penalties, if introduced later, cannot be resisted and need separate balance.

Selection weight remains x0.35 before base/region biases. These are provisional
allocations, not tested balance guarantees. No tests were run for this revision.
Existing generated records and unique designs are not rewritten.
