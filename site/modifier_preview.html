Rules & Profiles

Roll Structure

Each eligible non-projectile item copy receives its own modification roll. A successful roll checks the unique chance first; otherwise it draws prefix-only, suffix-only or both. Default layout weights are 1 / 1 / 1. Dual affixes have independent tiers.

Projectile Stacks

Arrows, bolts and thrown weapons roll once per original stack. A successful roll replaces the entire quantity with one fixed quality prefix: Honed (+1 damage, 80%), Keen (+2, 18%) or Piercing (+3, 2%). Zero damage channels stay zero; positive channels are capped at 255. There are no suffixes, dual affixes, enchantments or unique drops. Base value and weight are retained. The same base and grade reuse a record; existing legacy projectile items are unchanged.

Once-Per-Save Uniques

Duplicate uniques are disabled by default for new saves. A unique roll selects from valid, undiscovered templates for the exact base item. If none remain, it forces a compatible prefix-and-suffix item, even when the normal dual-affix weight is zero. If no compatible pair exists, the original item remains.

A unique is consumed after successful insertion into corpse loot, whether or not the player picks it up. Discovery survives save/load; selling or destroying the item does not reset it. Console samples do not consume discovery. Existing saved settings take precedence: turn off Allow Duplicate Unique Drops in older saves.

Modifier Types

TypeProperties
CraftedOne base-item property change, such as damage, armor, weight, value or reach.
MagicalOne spell effect: constant effect, cast on use or cast on strike.
HybridTwo or more effects within one affix; benefits, drawbacks or mixed property/spell changes.

Classification applies per affix, not per item. A prefix and suffix together do not automatically make an item Hybrid. Hybrid takes precedence for multi-effect affixes.

Hybrid Trade-Off Affixes

Six bargain families each occupy one affix slot and contain two effects. Constant effects pair a wearer benefit with a weakness or reversible attribute drain. On-strike effects pair a target debuff with a target buff: Hobbling Fury drains Speed but fortifies Strength; Blinding Vigor blinds but fortifies Agility. Both target effects last three seconds.

Benefits and costs scale through six tiers. Bargains are enabled by default, with family weight x0.35 before item and regional biases. Opposing drain/fortify and resistance/weakness effects across paired affixes are rejected, as are mixed enchantment modes. Strike bargains use native charge costs and exclude bows, crossbows and projectiles.

Every tier has its own affix name. At T6, clean Speechcraft/Enchant grants +6; bargains grant +8 with a 4-point Strength or 3-point Speed drain. Clean frost/shock resistance reaches 15%; bargains reach 20% with 10% elemental weakness. Strike bargains drain target Speed by 8 while granting 4 Strength, or inflict 16% Blind while granting 4 Agility, for three seconds.

Guaranteed Bargain Samples

In the OpenMW console, enter luag, then run either command:

require('openmw.interfaces').RandomisedBasicLoot.giveBargainSamples('iron_helmet', 1)
require('openmw.interfaces').RandomisedBasicLoot.giveBargainSamples('iron longsword', 6)

Lua syntax checks and the focused runtime harness pass. OpenMW engine behavior and balance still need in-game testing. Generation remains limited to NPC corpses; containers, merchants and relic-condition changes are outside this build.

Composition & Eligibility

  • Physical percentages use the original base stat. Maximum condition changes preserve the item's condition fraction.
  • Final weight determines armor class before an armor-skill suffix is selected. The three armor skills are conditional candidates, not simultaneous bonuses.
  • Duplicate effect families and mixed native enchantment modes cannot form a dual-affix item.
  • Enchant capacity combined with a native enchantment is intentionally allowed even when it becomes a dead modifier.
  • Only approved IDs in enabled source packs can roll. Scripted, already enchanted or otherwise protected records remain excluded.
  • Advanced utility families and Appraisal have separate configuration gates.
